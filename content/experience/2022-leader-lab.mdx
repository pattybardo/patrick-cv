## The Role

Leader Lab is a Gothenburg-based consultancy focused on developing leadership capability in technical organisations. As a Partner, I worked with clients on leadership development programmes, coaching, and organisational strategy — running in parallel with my engineering work at Meltwater.

## What This Work Involved

Working at the intersection of technical expertise and leadership development gave me a perspective on engineering organisations that purely hands-on engineering rarely does. The most effective technical leaders aren't the best individual contributors — they're the people who understand how to create the conditions for others to do their best work.

Consulting engagements covered engineering leadership coaching, helping managers transition from individual contributor to people leadership roles, and working with technical teams on communication and decision-making frameworks.

## Why It Mattered

The lens this work provided shaped how I approach platform engineering: the goal isn't to build impressive systems in isolation, but to build systems that make other engineering teams more effective. A platform team is, at its core, a leadership function — you're setting standards, removing friction, and creating leverage for the teams that build on top of you.
