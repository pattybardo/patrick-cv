## The Problem We Were Solving

Vanta Labs built real-time customer analytics for mid-market SaaS companies. The pitch was: give us your event stream and we'll give you actionable cohort analysis in under 30 seconds.

When I joined, that 30-second SLA was being met about 60% of the time. The pipeline was a brittle chain of Node.js workers bolted together with cron jobs and good intentions.

## What I Built

### The Ingestion Rework

Replaced the cron-based batch processor with a Kafka-backed streaming pipeline. Events are now durably buffered, schema-validated at ingest, and fanned out to downstream consumers without coupling.

The critical design decision was **idempotency keys at every stage**. The old system had silent duplicates that corrupted cohort counts. The new system can replay any window without side effects.

### The Search Layer

Rewrote the cohort query engine to push computation into Elasticsearch rather than pulling raw documents into Node and filtering in-memory. Median query time dropped from 8.2s to 340ms.

Built a query planner in TypeScript that translates our DSL into ES aggregation pipelines — the kind of thing that sounds straightforward until you're debugging why a time-zone-aware histogram doesn't match a client's spreadsheet at 11pm.

## Numbers

- **4.2M** events/day at peak, P99 latency < 200ms
- **99.1%** pipeline uptime over 18 months
- Search P50: **340ms** (from 8.2s)
- Team grew from 3 to 9 engineers during my tenure; I ran the backend chapter from month 18 onward
